# Requirements

Every decision in a depot carries a declaration: a legal name, a contact
email, and a confirmation that the person read this page, accepts it, and
that what they submit meets it. The declaration is public and nobody
checks it. It is a promise made under that name.

## Who declares

You are a real person, and the name you give is your legal name. The email
reaches you. You act in good faith: you do not add texts, decide, report
problems or ask for erasures to disrupt a depot, mislead its readers or
harass anyone. A decision says what you found after checking, never a
guess.

## What you add or admit

- Its licenses are on the allowed list, and you read them yourself
  ([licenses.md](licenses.md), "What the corpus accepts").
- It follows every rule of [licenses.md](licenses.md), "Rules".
- Its notice holds the copyright line and NOTICE content its license asks
  to keep, or the proof that it is in the public domain.
- Its owner has not refused AI training.
- It is not made of personal data.
- It is not output of another company's language model
  ([licenses.md](licenses.md), "No distillation from another company's
  model").
- When you are unsure, it stays under review.

## Admitting is human

A program or an AI assistant may add texts, query a depot and build
corpora for you. Admitting or refusing is your own decision, and the
declaration on it is yours.

## Erasure requests

A request names its passages by place and gives its real reason
([erasure.md](erasure.md)).
